    Feature Parity

    Which user-facing features each Voyager client has. The web column comes from this repository; the Android column from the Android app's README (status "every feature module is merged and integrated on main") as of 2026-11-13 (Phases 1 to 5 merged on main, plus the final-audit parity fixes); iOS does not exist yet. Phase numbers are from the design spec (section 11). Update this page in the same change that adds a client feature.

    Legend: ✅ shipped · ⏳ Phase N planned · — not offered · ? not stated in the Android README, so not confirmed.

    Known gaps

    • Android: background geofence arrival notification (not built, by ruling), (weather push, Deal-hunter track, bucket ring, passport stamp and drawn scratch map are now built.)
    • Android: one-tap watch on a saved plan or chat (when live prices and Alerts v2 are on), a dismissible watch offer right after a plan is saved, and Recap "Where next" (AI picks from /api/discover, on tap, seeded by the Taste Graph server-side) shipped in #121.
    • Android: follows, comments, head-to-head and the weekly challenge remain Phase 8.
